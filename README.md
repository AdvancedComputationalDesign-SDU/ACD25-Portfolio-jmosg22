# ACD-E25 Portfolio
## Overview

This portfolio documents four computational design studies developed for Advanced Computational Design. Across the series, I investigate how geometric logic, fields, and local rules can generate complex spatial and visual systems through Python (NumPy), recursive algorithms, Grasshopper/GhPython, and agent-based modeling, iterating through prototypes, parameter sweeps, and visual evaluation.

The work moves from 2D image-based pattern generation to recursive geometry, image-driven architectural systems, and finally agent-based surface rationalization, progressively increasing in spatial, geometric, and behavioral complexity.

## Assignments
### A1: NumPy Array Manipulation for 2D Pattern Generation

In A1, I develop a pixel-based pattern generator using NumPy array operations to transform a blank canvas into structured, colorized images. The assignment explores color interaction through sine-wave–driven gradients, controlled randomness, and rule-based compositional logic. By varying amplitude, frequency, resolution, and randomly seeded RGB values, I generate a family of related but distinct visual outcomes.

### A2: Exploring Fractals through Recursive Geometric Patterns

In A2, I build a recursive triangle-based geometric system to explore fractal logic, self-similarity, and hierarchical growth. Using recursion depth, rotation increments, and color interpolation mapped to depth, I study how small parameter changes affect density, legibility, and visual complexity. The work highlights the exponential nature of recursion and its computational and geometric implications.

### A3: Parametric Structural Canopy

In A3, I design a parametric architectural canopy system driven by a NumPy-generated heightmap and implemented through Grasshopper and GhPython. Image-derived heightfields are reconstructed as NURBS surfaces, tessellated adaptively based on curvature, and structurally supported by recursively generated branching columns. The project integrates surface analysis, structural logic, adaptive panelization, and variable-radius supports into a single procedural pipeline.

### A4: Agent-Based Modeling for Surface Panelization

In A4, I develop an agent-based system that samples and rationalizes a surface into a panelized structure. Agents operate in UV space, responding to Gaussian curvature as an attractor and inter-agent distance as a repulsive constraint. Through iterative simulation and persistent agent states, local behaviors produce a globally coherent tessellation, with dense panels in high-curvature regions and larger panels in flatter areas.

## Highlights:

- **Technique:** Recursive and rule-based systems applied consistently across image generation, geometry, structure, and agent behavior

- **Best result:** The integrated canopy system with adaptive tessellation and branching supports (A3)

- **Next step:** Extending the agent-based framework to incorporate fabrication constraints such as panel size limits, material behavior, or structural feedback
